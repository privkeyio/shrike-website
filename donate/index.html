Donate

Shrike is free and open source, and always will be.

If you would like to support the work, email donate@shrikewallet.com and you will get an address back.

Why there is no address on this page

One published address means every donation lands on the same place, so the running total and each individual contribution stay linked and public for good. Handing out an address per person keeps what you sent to yourself. A page that does this without the email round trip is being worked on.

Shrike has not been audited and comes with no warranty of any kind. It is an unofficial fork of Sparrow Bitcoin Wallet, licensed under the Apache 2.0 license, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Sparrow project.
